A rare outcome

Class weighting gives subscribers more influence during training. It does not turn the model score into a calibrated probability.

Global importance ≠ local explanation

Feature importance summarizes how much a feature reduces impurity across the whole tree. It does not say whether that feature raises or lowers an individual prediction.

SHAP explains a single profile: positive contributions raise its score from the model baseline; negative contributions lower it.

No call-duration shortcut

Duration is only known after a call, so it is excluded from this pre-call model.

Association, not cause

Changing a feature in the explorer changes the model output. It does not prove it changes customer behavior.

Historical campaign effects

Month, day and contact channel may reflect past campaign practices that change over time.

Limits of importance

Impurity importance can favor features with many possible splits. Correlated signals can share importance.
