What do the metrics mean?

Accuracy

How often the model is correct across all customers.

Precision

Of customers predicted to subscribe, how many actually did?

Recall

Of all actual subscribers, how many did the model find?

F1 score

The harmonic mean of precision and recall, balancing the two.

REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

Final model configuration

55 candidate settings were compared with five validation folds on the training data. Each fold used separate three-fold tuning of the decision cutoff. The winner was selected by mean validation F1.

The test split has been inspected across project revisions. These are same-split comparisons, not a fresh independent audit or a guarantee of future performance.
